# Borang Permohonan Keahlian - User Guide

## Overview

Borang Permohonan Keahlian is the official membership application form for DPMM Negeri Johor. This guide walks you through the 7-step application process.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
- MyKAD number
- SSM business registration certificate
- Business registration documents
- Passport-sized photo
- Payment slip (for membership fee)

## The Application Process

The form has 7 steps that take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete.

## Step 1: Landing Page

![Landing Page](./borang-step1-landing.png)

**What you see:**
- DPMM Negeri Johor logo and header
- "Borang Permohonan Keahlian" title
- AIMAN chatbot assistant (bottom right)
- Business type selection dropdown
- Two fill options: "Isi Pintar" and "Isi Manual"

**Instructions:**
1. Select your business type from the dropdown:
   - Enterprise (Milikan Tunggal)
   - Enterprise (Perkongsian)
   - Sdn Bhd (100% Melayu)
   - Professional Berdaftar
   - Koperasi (100% Melayu)
2. Click "Isi Pintar" for AI-assisted filling (recommended)
3. Click "Isi Manual" to fill the form yourself

**Tips:**
- "Isi Pintar" uses AI to automatically fill in your business information from SSM
- "Isi Manual" requires you to fill each field manually
- Have your MyKAD and SSM number ready before starting

## Step 2: Entity Information

After selecting your business type, you'll be asked to provide:

**Required Information:**
- Full business name (from SSM)
- Business registration number
- MyKAD number
- Passport-sized photo upload
- Contact information (phone, email)

**Instructions:**
1. Enter your business name exactly as shown in SSM certificate
2. Enter your business registration number
3. Enter your MyKAD number (format: XXXXXX-XX-XXXX)
4. Upload your passport-sized photo (JPG/PNG, max 2MB)
5. Enter your phone number and email address
6. Click "Seterusnya" to proceed

**Tips:**
- Double-check all information against your SSM certificate
- Ensure your photo meets passport photo requirements
- Use a valid email address for notifications

## Step 3: Business Details

Provide detailed information about your business:

**Required Information:**
- Business address
- Postal code
- City/State
- Business sector/industry
- Number of employees
- Annual turnover
- Business description

**Instructions:**
1. Fill in your complete business address
2. Select your business sector from the dropdown
3. Enter number of employees
4. Enter annual turnover
5. Write a brief description of your business activities
6. Click "Seterusnya" to proceed

**Tips:**
- Be accurate with your business description
- Select the most appropriate sector
- Include all sources of income in turnover calculation

## Step 4: Shareholders Information

Provide information about business owners and directors:

**Required Information:**
- Owner/director name
- MyKAD number
- Position
- Phone number
- Email address
- Shareholding percentage

**Instructions:**
1. Add each shareholder/director
2. Enter their MyKAD number
3. Specify their position
4. Enter contact information
5. Set shareholding percentage
6. Click "Tambah" to add more shareholders
7. Click "Seterusnya" when complete

**Tips:**
- Include all major shareholders (more than 20% ownership)
- Ensure total shareholding equals 100%
- Verify all MyKAD numbers are correct

## Step 5: Document Upload

Upload required supporting documents:

**Required Documents:**
- SSM business registration certificate
- Latest annual return
- Passport-sized photo
- Other relevant business documents

**Instructions:**
1. Click "Muat naik" for each required document
2. Select the file from your computer
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Ensure all documents are uploaded successfully
5. Click "Seterusnya" to proceed

**Tips:**
- Use clear, readable copies of documents
- Ensure files are in PDF or image format
- Maximum file size is typically 5MB per document
- Have all documents ready before starting

## Step 6: Payment

Pay the membership fee:

**Payment Methods:**
- Online bank transfer
- Credit/debit card
- QR code payment

**Instructions:**
1. Select your preferred payment method
2. Follow the payment instructions
3. Complete the payment
4. Upload payment slip
5. Click "Seterusnya" to proceed

**Tips:**
- Keep your payment slip as proof of payment
- Note your reference number
- Payment processing may take 1-2 business days

## Step 7: Review and Submit

Review all information before final submission:

**Review Checklist:**
- Verify all personal information is correct
- Check business details are accurate
- Ensure all documents are uploaded
- Confirm payment slip is uploaded
- Review terms and conditions

**Instructions:**
1. Review all sections for accuracy
2. Make any necessary corrections
3. Read and accept terms and conditions
4. Click "Hantar Permohonan" to submit
5. Note your reference number for tracking

**Tips:**
- Take your time to review all information
- Your reference number will be displayed after submission
- You'll receive email confirmation
- Processing typically takes 3-5 business days

## AIMAN Assistant

AIMAN (Ahli Inovasi & Maju Aspirasi Negeri) is your digital assistant:

**How to Use AIMAN:**
- Click the AIMAN button (bottom right)
- Type your question in English or Malay
- AIMAN will respond in the same language
- Ask about:
  - Application process
  - Membership requirements
  - Business guidance
  - Troubleshooting

**AIMAN Features:**
- English and Malay language support
- Conversational and helpful tone
- 100-word response limit for clarity
- Available during all form steps

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Form Not Loading:**
- Check your internet connection
- Refresh the page
- Try a different browser

**Document Upload Failing:**
- Check file size (max 5MB)
- Ensure file format is supported (PDF, JPG, PNG)
- Try a different browser

**Payment Issues:**
- Check your bank account balance
- Verify payment details
- Contact support if payment fails

**AIMAN Not Responding:**
- Refresh the page
- Check internet connection
- Try asking a simpler question

### Getting Help

If you encounter issues:
1. Check this guide for solutions
2. Ask AIMAN for assistance
3. Contact DPMM Negeri Johor support
4. Visit DPMM office for in-person assistance

## After Submission

**What Happens Next:**

3. Approval typically takes 3-5 business days
4. You'll receive membership certificate upon approval
