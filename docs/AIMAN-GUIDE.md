# AIMAN Guide

## What is AIMAN?

AIMAN (Ahli Inovasi & Maju Aspirasi Negeri) is the official digital assistant and business guide for DPMM Negeri Johor. It helps users navigate the membership application process, provides business guidance, and answers questions about the system.

## Language Support

AIMAN supports both **Bahasa Melayu** and **English**:

- **Bahasa Melayu:** Default language for Malaysian users
- **English:** Responds in English when you ask in English

### How to Use English

Simply ask your question in English, and AIMAN will respond in English:

```
User: "How do I apply for membership?"
AIMAN: "To apply for membership, please visit the membership application form..."
```

```
User: "Apakah cara untuk memohon keahlian?"
AIMAN: "Untuk memohon keahlian, sila layari borang permohonan..."
```

## Persona and Tone

AIMAN is designed to be:
- **Conversational:** Friendly and approachable, not robotic
- **Helpful:** Provides clear, actionable guidance
- **Professional:** Maintains business etiquette
- **Empowering:** Focuses on helping entrepreneurs succeed

### Communication Style

- Uses simple, clear language
- Avoids legal jargon without explanation
- Respects small and micro entrepreneurs
- Provides actionable steps
- Limited to 100 words per response for clarity

## Common Questions

### About Membership
- "How do I apply for membership?"
- "What are the membership fees?"
- "What documents do I need?"

### About Business
- "How can I grow my business?"
- "What government support is available?"
- "How do I register my business?"

### About the System
- "Where can I find my application status?"
- "How do I update my information?"
- "Who do I contact for help?"

## Troubleshooting

### AIMAN Not Responding
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page
3. Ensure the chatbot widget is open
4. Try asking a simpler question

### Language Detection Issues
- If AIMAN responds in the wrong language, rephrase your question with clearer language indicators
- Use complete sentences for better detection

### Response Quality
- If responses seem generic, provide more specific context
- AIMAN works best with clear, specific questions
- For complex issues, AIMAN may direct you to official DPMM staff

## Limitations

- AIMAN does not have access to private member data
- AIMAN cannot make changes to your application
- AIMAN cannot process payments
- For sensitive data queries, AIMAN will direct you to DPMM staff

## Best Practices

- Be specific in your questions
- Provide context when needed
- Use clear language
- Ask one question at a time
- Follow up if you need clarification
